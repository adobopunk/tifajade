Essays put the writing first. The text is set in a readable serif column,
and photos can be dropped in between paragraphs from the CMS.

![A dancer in a feathered headdress](/assets/img/projects/good-loser/may-day-dancer.webp)

## Headings break up longer pieces

Use them for sections. Quotes stand out on their own:

> Sometimes the most important work is done by people who know they may not
> prevail immediately.

Photos added in the **Photos** list appear after the text, in the same
layouts a photo story uses.
